Our Story

A table full of flavour, a feeling of home

Spice Garden began with a simple idea: the best meals are the ones you want to share. We bring the warmth of Indian kitchens to a menu that travels from South Indian classics to Chinese and Continental favourites.

Every dish is made to order with fresh ingredients, thoughtful seasoning and the kind of care that makes a quick lunch feel like a little occasion. Come as you are; there is always room at our table.
